## 5. FAILURES found — LaTeX escaping defects (would break pdfLaTeX compilation)

- **Unescaped `_` in 19/23 files** (headers `roc_auc`, `balanced_acc`, `topk_jaccard`;
  labels `case_random`, `data_integrity`, …) → "Missing $ inserted" errors.
- **Raw `%` in 5 files** (gates, leakage_analysis, negative_results, representation_analysis,
  statistical_tests) — e.g. gates.tex:9 `rate 0.19820% <= 0.100%` comments out the row
  terminator and breaks the table.
- **Raw `&` inside cell content in 3 files / 10 rows** → column misalignment ("Extra
  alignment tab"): top_grams_logodds_phish rows 1,2,3,21–24,27 (n-grams `bmit&`, `mit&i`,
  `ls&s`, `s&ses`…), top_grams_logodds_legit row 14 (`pe=2&`), sanity_checks row 2
  (`True & True` → 5 cells vs 4-col spec). Content also renders corrupted.
- **Raw `#`** in top_grams_logodds_legit (n-gram `#com`, row 14).
- **Non-ASCII `∩` (5 files) and `—` (2 files)** — undefined outside math mode under
  pdfLaTeX + utf8 inputenc.
- Only **3/23 files are compile-clean** (cleaning_ledger, data_audit, partitioning).

## Verdict

**CONDITIONAL FAIL.** Data fidelity and structure are excellent (23/23 manifest-matched,
row/column counts exact, no truncation), but the LaTeX exporter does not escape `_ % # &`
or sanitize non-ASCII, so 20/23 tables will not compile under pdfLaTeX as-is. A dedicated
escaping pass (escape `\_\%\#\&`, render `∩` as `$\cap$`, `—` as `---`, drop `Unnamed: 0`,
consider `<1e-4` for underflowed p-values) is required before paper build. Infrastructure-
only fix; no scientific content change implied.
